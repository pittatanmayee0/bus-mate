Detailed System Architecture

How BusMate connects students, drivers, and institutions into a single real-time transit network.

📱 Student & Parent Mobile Experience

The mobile portal eliminates schedule ambiguity through continuous telemetry tracking, automated geofences, and active alert systems.

  • Live Coordinate Stream: Maps refresh location vectors every 3 seconds for smooth visual movement.
  • 5-Minute Geofence Alarm: Automatic background notifications wake students up right as the bus enters their specific neighborhood radius.
  • Silent Attendance Toggle: Students can tap "Skip Pickup Today" to prevent drivers from stopping at empty locations.
  • Masked Driver Calls: Enables necessary voice calls without exposing personal phone numbers.

👨‍✈️ Hands-Free Driver Mobile Interface

Designed with absolute safety in mind, drivers never interact with complex UI while operating the vehicle.

  • One-Tap Route Initiation: Drivers press a single start button at the beginning of the trip to broadcast GPS data automatically.
  • Audio-Assisted Stop Alerts: Voice prompts inform drivers of skipped stops when students mark themselves absent in advance.
  • Emergency Broadcast: A dedicated button sends immediate breakdown or accident alerts to the school administration center.

🏫 Central School Administration Portal

Gives logistics managers total control over their entire transportation operations, driver schedules, and vehicle health.

  • Fleet Diagnostics: Monitors speed, signal strength, off-route deviations, and trip completion metrics.
  • Dynamic Route Assignment: Instantaneously reassigns swapped buses or drivers to pre-mapped routes.
  • Security Controls: Role-based access keys ensure student location data stays strictly confidential.
  • Parent Broadcast Channel: Send campus-wide announcements regarding weather cancellations or holiday schedules.
